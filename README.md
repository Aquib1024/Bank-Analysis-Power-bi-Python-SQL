## 📋 Executive Summary
This project delivers a comprehensive end-to-end data analysis solution for a banking institution. By leveraging **SQL** for data handling, **Python** for Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), and **Power BI** for visualization, I analyzed a robust dataset of **3,000 client records**.

The final dashboard provides strategic insights into a portfolio consisting of **£4.38bn in total loans** and **£3.77bn in total deposits**, enabling stakeholders to identify cross-selling opportunities and optimize client segmentation.

---

## 💼 Business Problem
The bank aimed to move beyond basic demographic segmentation to understand the true drivers of customer value. The analysis focused on:
1.  **Product Penetration:** Identifying opportunities to cross-sell credit cards to existing clients.
2.  **Customer Engagement:** Determining which behaviors correlate most strongly with high deposit balances.
3.  **Risk & Segmentation:** Analyzing the client base across income, occupation, and loyalty tiers.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Workflow

* **Data Source:** SQL Database (extracted 3,000 records with 25 variables).
* **Data Quality:** Achieved pristine data quality with **0 missing values** across all fields.
* **Exploratory Analysis (EDA):** Python (Pandas, Seaborn) used to calculate correlations and demographic distributions.
* **Visualization:** Microsoft Power BI (Interactive Dashboard with bookmarks, drill-downs, and dynamic filtering).
* **Reporting:** PowerPoint (Synthesis of strategic insights).

---

## 🔍 Key Findings & Insights

### 1. Cross-Selling Opportunity 💳
* **Single Product Dominance:** **64%** of clients maintain only one credit card.
* **Strategy:** This segment represents the largest opportunity for targeted cross-selling campaigns to expand product holding.
* **Premium Segment:** Only **10.4%** of clients maximize their portfolio with three cards.

### 2. Behavioral Correlations 📈
* **Engagement predicts Value:** There is a remarkably strong correlation of **0.84** between Bank Deposits and Checking Accounts, and **0.75** with Savings Accounts.
* **Income is Secondary:** Estimated income shows only a weak-to-moderate correlation (**0.26-0.37**) with financial variables. This indicates that financial behavior is a better predictor of engagement than salary alone.

### 3. Demographics & Portfolio 👥
* **Gender Parity:** The client base is perfectly balanced, with **50.4% female clients** and minimal gender disparity across income or fees.
* **Occupation Diversity:** The bank serves clients across **195 unique occupations**, ranging from Nurses to Nuclear Power Engineers.
* **Income Distribution:** The core client segment (50.6%) falls into the Medium Income bracket (£100k - £300k).

## 🚀 Strategic Recommendations

1.  **Activate Single-Card Holders:** Launch tailored campaigns targeting the 64% of clients holding a single card to increase wallet share.
2.  **Behavior-Based Products:** Develop products that reward active fund management (transfers between checking/savings) rather than just high income, leveraging the strong 0.84 correlation.
3.  **Occupation Bundles:** Create specific financial packages for top occupations to address the diverse professional backgrounds (195 unique roles) found in the data.
